The short manual

Support

Connect the desk. Save your heights. Keep Stop close.

Standing Desk is an independent iPhone utility for the IKEA IDÅSEN desk and other LINAK-based Bluetooth sit-stand desks.

Keep the physical controller within reach. Use it to stop unexpected desk movement.

Connect a desk

  1. 1Open Settings in Standing Desk.
  2. 2Put the desk in Bluetooth pairing mode.
  3. 3Select Scan for Desks.
  4. 4Select the correct desk explicitly.

Common fixes

  • Enable Bluetooth for Standing Desk in iPhone Settings.
  • Stay close to the desk during pairing.
  • Quit other applications that control the same desk.
  • Keep the app open during movement.
  • Use Forget Desk, then scan again if the saved selection is stale.
