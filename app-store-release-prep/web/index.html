Before you download

Made for IKEA IDÅSEN.

Standing Desk speaks the LINAK Bluetooth protocol used by the IKEA IDÅSEN desk. IDÅSEN is verified; other LINAK-based desks may also work. It does not fit every sit-stand desk, and setup only offers desks the app recognizes. Keep the physical desk controller within reach whenever the desk moves.
